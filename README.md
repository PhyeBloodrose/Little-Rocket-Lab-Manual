# How to Play this Manual
Start a new game and just enjoy the game! Checks are part of the main quest and the various "side quests" you can do with other characters.

It doesn't matter if you pick normal or hard mode. Hard Mode just makes the grind longer! As everything just require more resources on Hard.

Anything you can craft are randomized. You need to receive the item before you're allowed to craft them.

Gear is also randomized, such as gloves and hats.

Some miscellenous items are also randomized.

Ores are only randomized if the yaml option is enabled for it.

## Chapters and Seasons
Since the game has different chapters all tied to a season, you can decide where the end will be depending on what Season you want to end at in the yaml. I decided to name the second Spring Season the finale to easier see the difference from the first chapter.

The goals are tied to chapter shifts or sending off the rocket if your chapter access is the finale!

## Yaml Options
- Friendsanity

You can have each heart of the townspeople be a check. This option will also add an item for each heart that is required before they get in logic as to not overwhelm anyone with the grind.

- Photo Album

Picking up Photos for your Photo Album exists as an optional way to get some checks.

- Town Hall Upgrades

As the Town Hall Upgrades cost a lot of money to unlock, I added them as an option for people who love that grind.
